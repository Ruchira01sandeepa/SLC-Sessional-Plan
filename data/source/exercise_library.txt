Session-time example
For a 60-minute U15 session, use:

Warm-up — 10 minutes: jogging, skipping, high knees, leg swings, arm circles, lateral shuffles.

Strength — 15 minutes: role-based exercises, such as split squats, push-ups, band rows, glute bridges, calf raises.

Power or speed — 10 minutes: broad jumps, medicine-ball throws, 10 m sprints, or cone drills.

Role-specific activity — 15 minutes: spin-bowling target drill, fast-bowling controlled net work, fielding circuit, keeping drill, or batting throwdowns.

Injury prevention — 5 minutes: shoulder band work, side plank, hamstring or groin exercise, balance drill.

Cool-down — 5 minutes: easy walking, breathing, and gentle stretching.

Keep bowling volumes low and progressive for U15 players, and prioritize controlled technique, landing mechanics, and recovery before intensity.
